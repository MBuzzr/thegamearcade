QUESTIONS

What's included?

120+ systems with thousands of videogames and pinball tables, an ever expanding collection.

How do I get the downloads?

Once you join, we will grant you access to the library for instant download.

Can I cancel? What about refunds?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Lifetime access is a one-time payment with no subscription.

Which devices and systems does it work on?

All our collections are built and pre-configured for Windows PCs. The game files themselves can also be used on other supported devices and emulators, though the ready-to-play setups are made for Windows. Not sure about your device? Message us on WhatsApp before you buy and we'll tell you what to expect.

How big are the downloads? How much space do I need?

Sizes depend on which systems you choose. You can download only the collections you want, so you don't need the whole archive. Ask us if you're unsure how much space to plan for.

Do I need to set up emulators myself?

The collections come pre-configured, so you shouldn't have to configure emulators by hand. If something doesn't work, members can message us for setup help.

How often is the library updated?

New additions are added as they're ready. Follow us on YouTube or Instagram to see what's new.
